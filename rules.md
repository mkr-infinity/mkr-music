# MKR Music — Reuse, Attribution & Project Rules

This document sets out the guidelines and attribution requirements for copying, adapting, or reusing any part of the **MKR Music** project in other applications, repositories, or services.

---

## 1. Open Source License Compliance

MKR Music is distributed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)**. Anyone modifying, distributing, or incorporating code from MKR Music must comply with all terms of the GPL-3.0 license:

- **Source Code Availability**: Any derivative work or application containing MKR Music code must also be fully open-sourced under the GPL-3.0 license.
- **Copyright Notices**: All existing copyright, license notices, and author disclaimers must be preserved in source files and repository documentation.
- **Third-Party Dependencies**: Third-party libraries, services, and assets integrated into MKR Music remain subject to their respective open-source licenses and attribution requirements. Reusers are responsible for adhering to those terms.

---

## 2. Attribution Requirements

Original work developed for MKR Music — including bespoke UI layouts, custom components, original artwork, branding, adaptive icon assets, and architectural implementations — represents substantial creative and engineering effort.

When reusing or adapting original MKR Music material:

1. **Prominent Visible Credit**: Provide clear, accessible credit to **MKR Music** and its author (**Mohammad Kaif Raja** / `@mkr-infinity`) in your project's `README.md`, About section, and repository documentation.
2. **No False Authorship**: Reusers must not present original MKR Music work, layouts, or assets as their own independent creation.
3. **No Implied Endorsement**: Providing attribution does not imply that MKR Music, its author, or contributors endorse, sponsor, or are affiliated with the derivative project.
4. **Trademark & Identity Protection**: Attribution requirements do not grant permission to use MKR Music's project name, logo, or distinctive branding in a manner that misleads users or causes brand confusion.

---

## 3. Enforcement & Takedown Policy

The project author reserves the right to review and investigate suspected unauthorized use of original MKR Music code, design assets, and materials:

- **Resolution Inquiries**: Where original work is used without required credit or in violation of license terms, the author may contact the maintainer, host, or publishing platform to request appropriate attribution, correction, or removal.
- **Formal Takedowns**: Where substantive infringement or license non-compliance persists and legal requirements are met, the project author may submit a formal copyright or DMCA takedown notice to hosting providers and app stores.
- **Scope**: This policy addresses substantive, unauthorized copying and misrepresentation of original work. It does not assert exclusive ownership over generic UI concepts or standard open-source patterns.
